 Terms & Conditions

01. Course Access & Duration Policy

Course access depends on the selected program: a 3-month course includes 6 months of total access, and a 2-month course includes 4 months of access. Additional access time may be extended upon request.

02. How It Works

The platform utilizes a self-paced learning model. Participants are expected to complete modules sequentially. Certain advanced courses may require the completion of prerequisite introductory materials before granting access to higher-tier content.

03. Deliverables

Each course includes high-definition video lectures, downloadable PDF summaries, and interactive self-assessment quizzes. A digital certificate of completion is provided upon passing all required assessments with a minimum score of 80%.

04. Payment & Activation Conditions

All fees are non-refundable once course access has been granted. Account activation is processed manually and typically occurs within 12-24 hours after valid payment proof is submitted via the designated WhatsApp channel. ENG-LISTON reserves the right to deny access if payment cannot be verified.
